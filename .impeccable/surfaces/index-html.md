# Surface: Riad BE Marrakech — single-page site

- Scope: full home page, one route (`/`, `index.html`).
- Visitor mode: Persuade.
- Audience: international leisure travelers comparing boutique riads in the Marrakech medina.
- Job: decide the place feels authentic and reachable, compare rooms and prices, start a reservation inquiry.
- Action: WhatsApp chat and the reservation request form (both preserved; no backend).
- Proof/content: four real room offers with prices, guest quotes, address, hours, spa/yoga/tours. Photography is placeholder stock and must not be presented as verified property photos.
- Constraints: static Vite + vanilla JS/CSS, mobile-first, WCAG 2.1 AA aim, reduced-motion support, no new runtime dependency.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the last turn of the walk to the riad — a painted medina-wayfinding world that turns the hidden-alley arrival problem into the reason to trust the house. It refuses the photo-hero-plus-equal-cards boutique template.

OWN-WORLD: Chalk limewash plaster and deep Majorelle-ultramarine wall fields, cut by glazed teal doors, ochre and tomato-red sign paint; chalk enamel plaques with black-painted display lettering; dashed route lines, bolted signplates, brass fittings. Recognisable with all copy removed.

STORY: The visitor recognises the real difficulty of finding a riad in the medina, feels this is a lived-in home reachable by a real person, understands the rooms, prices and what is included, and starts a reservation or a WhatsApp conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds, a chalk-painted wall carrying an oversized painted RIAD BE sign and a hand-painted arrival route (airport → Bab Doukkala → derb → the door); right third, a teal door set in a whitewashed arch with a working arrival plaque (check-in, check-out, guests, room) and a tomato-red request action. The dashed route continues below the fold as the page's spine.
